Privacy

EDF Tracker runs entirely in your web browser. The invoices, client names, amounts and filing records you enter are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else: the site has no server-side code, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and its content security policy blocks the page from making network requests.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP address) when your browser downloads the page files; see GitHub's privacy statement. Downloaded backups, CSV and calendar files are saved wherever you choose and are your responsibility to keep safe. Clearing your browser data, or using “Delete all data”, permanently removes your records from this device.
