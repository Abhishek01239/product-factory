What changed on 1 October 2026

Every service export needs an EDF

Under Regulation 3(2) of the FEMA (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026, exporters of services declare the full export value on an Export Declaration Form. There is no minimum invoice size. SOFTEX is gone; software exports use the EDF too.

Due 30 days after month-end

File within 30 days from the end of the month in which you raised the invoice. October invoices: 30 November. November: 30 December. December: 30 January 2027. One EDF can cover every invoice of the month, across all clients.

Get paid within 9 months

The full value should be realised within 9 months of the invoice date (12 months if invoiced or settled in rupees). Your bank can extend either deadline if you ask with reasons, ideally before it passes.

Small invoices close easily

For invoices up to ₹10 lakh, your bank can close the entry on your own declaration that payment was received, filed singly or as a quarterly bulk declaration.

Non-software services: the regulations also let you file on or before the day payment arrives. Guidance differs on whether that replaces the 30-day limit, so this tracker conservatively shows the 30-day date as your outer deadline. Confirm with your bank.

FAQ

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Invoices are stored in your browser's local storage on this device only. The page has no server, no cookies, no analytics, and a security policy that blocks network requests. Use “Download backup” to move data between devices.

Does this file the EDF for me?

No. You file the EDF with your Authorised Dealer (AD) bank, or STPI/SEZ authority where applicable. The tracker tells you when, and exports a CSV laid out like Part 2B of the form (recipient, invoice, currency, amount, net realisable value, contract, description, SAC) so you can fill it quickly.

I get paid via Upwork, Wise, PayPal or Skydo. Does this apply?

Published guidance says to ask which AD bank the money actually settles through, and file the EDF with that bank. Bank procedures were still being finalised in early October 2026, so check your bank's published SOP.

What about invoices dated before 1 October 2026?

The new EDF requirement applies to exports from 1 October 2026. Transitional treatment of older invoices paid later is not clearly stated, so the tracker labels them “before new rules” and you should check with your bank.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. It is a free calculator and organiser based on the published regulations and practitioner guidance listed below. Rules and bank procedures can change; verify important deadlines with your bank or a chartered accountant.
