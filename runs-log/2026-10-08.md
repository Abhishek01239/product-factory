# Product Factory run: 2026-10-08

Decision: **IMPROVE** (EDF Tracker v1.0.0 → v1.1.0)

## Why IMPROVE instead of BUILD
On 7 Oct 2026 (the day after launch) RBI's Governor and Deputy Governor said at a press conference that individuals with forex
transactions of a personal nature, including people providing services abroad such as tutoring or small software work, are not
required to report; that small exporters with bills up to ₹10 lakh each can use a self-declaration with the invoice; that banks do the
portal reporting; and that FAQs will follow (BusinessLine, New Indian Express, TaxGuru, all 7 Oct). The live product's headline said
"every Indian freelancer ... must file", which was now misleading. Correcting a live product and answering the new top question
("do I still need to file?") beat every new idea found.
